Spark Lender Status

Lender status by store for Spark leadership

Put the Spark icon on this device
  1. iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  2. Android: in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen.
  3. Computer: in Chrome or Edge, open the menu, then Cast, save and share (or Apps), then Create shortcut or Install.

After setup, the Spark icon opens the app directly. Sign in to claude.ai the first time if asked.
